# Historical Askora brand guidelines

This document preserves the former identity for historical phase reports. The active product identity is documented in [STATOMERA brand identity](BRAND_IDENTITY.md).

**Askora** is the product name. Use lowercase **askora** in the wordmark. The supplied *Askora Electric Graphite Brand Board.png* shows the approved ribbon-like A with a rising line and three connected circular nodes, a rounded lowercase wordmark, a standalone rounded-square icon and a simplified monochrome variant. “AI Data Analyst” appears on the board as a subtitle. Product tagline remains open.

## Electric Graphite palette

| Role | Hex |
| --- | --- |
| Main background | #141617 |
| Sidebar and cards | #242627 |
| Elevated surfaces | #373C3F |
| Secondary gray | #99A3A6 |
| Primary accent, Champagne Gold | #D6B56E |
| Primary text | #F5F7FA |

Use dark by default with restrained gold emphasis. Electric Blue #0A87FA is historical and its secondary chart role remains open. Light mode is optional. Provide semantic labels or shapes alongside chart and status colors. Test the final text, focus and interactive states for contrast.

Preserve the A geometry, graph and wordmark in separately approved vector, monochrome and icon exports. Use the presentation board as reference. Validate the mark at favicon scale and across approved surfaces. Favor readable type, consistent spacing and restrained transitions. Avoid decorative glow in the product UI.

Inter and IBM Plex Sans Arabic are font candidates. Evaluate license and fallbacks, Arabic shaping and diacritics, mixed-language headings, numeral alignment, narrow-screen legibility and chart labels before finalizing.
